🎒 Packing

  • Cover shoulders & knees (temples)
  • Slip-on shoes (removed constantly)
  • Compact umbrella (daily carry)
  • SPF 50+ · Insect repellent
  • Electrolyte sachets
  • Refillable water bottle

🙏 Temple Rules

  • Remove shoes inside buildings
  • Never point feet at Buddha
  • Don't touch monks (esp. women)
  • Walk clockwise around chedis
  • Ask before photographing monks
  • Small donation ฿20-50

💡 Practical

  • Cash is king - small bills ฿20-100
  • Complete TDAC 72 hrs before landing
  • Grab app works for rides
  • 7-Eleven: water, SIM cards
  • "Sawasdee" (hello) · "Khob khun" (thanks)

🌡 Daily Rhythm

  • 6:30-10 AM: Walk & temples
  • 10-12: Museums (air-con)
  • 12-3 PM: Lunch, rest, massage
  • 3-6 PM: Second walk
  • 6 PM+: Markets & dinner
