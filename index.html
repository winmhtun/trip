Check In & Nimman Walk

One Nimman: open-air complex with vintage-industrial charm, pop-up shops, live music. Maya Mall: modern mall with rooftop terrace & mountain views. Both walkable.

Day 02
Old City & Markets
🚶 Walking day
🛒 Markets
🌙 Sunday Walking Street
Old City · Warorot · Ratchadamnoen
8:00 AM — Temples
Old City Temple Walk
Explore the moat-enclosed Old City. Hit Wat Chedi Luang (ruined 82m chedi, free Monk Chat), Wat Phra Singh (finest Lanna murals), and Wat Phan Tao (teak temple). All walkable.
11:00 AM — Market
Warorot Market
📍 Wichayanon Rd · Map
Chiang Mai's oldest public market. Ground floor: dried fruit, spices, local snacks. Upper floors: textiles. Try the mango sticky rice and Thai iced tea. Open daily 6 AM–5 PM.
1:00–5:00 PM — Rest
Hotel + Coffee
Parents: Rest or Thai massage. Younger group: Explore specialty coffee — Akha Ama or Ristr8to.
5:00 PM — Night Market
Sunday Walking Street
📍 Ratchadamnoen Rd · Map
Chiang Mai's legendary Sunday market. Entire Old City main road closes for hundreds of vendors. Crafts, textiles, silver, street food. Arrive 5 PM — packed by 7. Sundays only.
Sunday Market
If Not Sunday

Visit the Saturday Walking Street on Wualai Rd or the Night Bazaar food courts.

Day 03
Sticky Waterfall & Cafés
🚗 Van day
📸 Instagram spots
☕ Café trail
Mae Rim · Si Lanna
8:00 AM — Nature
Bua Tong Sticky Waterfall
📍 Si Lanna NP (~1 hr) · Map
Walk UP a waterfall. Calcium carbonate rocks are naturally grippy. No special shoes needed. Kids to grandparents can do it. Lockers ฿30. Free entry. Arrive before 9 AM. Closes 4:30 PM.
12:00 PM — Lunch
Carp Café
📍 Hang Dong · Map
Dine surrounded by giant koi fish. Sit on sunken islands with carp swimming around you. Feed fish with baby bottles (฿20–40). Even the bathroom has aquarium walls. ฿150–300/person.
Carp CaféChom Café
2:30 PM — Café
Chom Café
📍 Mae Hia · Map
Fairytale garden with waterfalls and mist. Stunning landscaping, live music on weekends. Great tom yum soup. ฿200–400/person.
4:30 PM
Natura Café
Pyramid-shaped café near CMU. Cool air-con retreat. Coffee & desserts before heading back.

Day 04
Mountain Drive
🚗 Van day
⛰ Sa Moeng Loop
🌅 Sunset at Ang Kaew
Sa Moeng · Mon Cham · CMU
8:00 AM — Scenic Drive
Sa Moeng Loop
One of Thailand's most beautiful drives. Winding mountain roads through forests and hill tribe villages. Stop at viewpoints. The loop itself is the attraction.
11:00 AM — Lunch
Mon Cham (Mon Jam)
📍 Mae Rim · Map
Mountain-top gardens with panoramic views. Flower beds, small cafés, the famous "hand in the sky" photo spot. Cooler up here. Entrance ฿50 per area.
2:30 PM — Relax
Huay Tung Tao Lake
📍 Map
Lakeside bamboo huts. Order food, relax with mountain backdrop. Very laid-back, mostly locals.
Lakeside
5:30 PM — Sunset
Ang Kaew Reservoir (CMU)
📍 Map
Chiang Mai's most beautiful sunset spot. The reservoir reflects Doi Suthep mountain. Free entry. Open 5 AM–10 PM.

Day 06
Nature Day — Mae Wang
🚗 Van day
🏞 National Park
🐘 Elephant option
Mae Wang National Park
8:00 AM — Nature
Mae Wang National Park
📍 ~1 hr south · Map
Pa Chor Cliffs — dramatic rock formations dubbed "Thailand's Grand Canyon." Easy walking trails, viewpoints, café. Entry ฿100 + ฿30 car.
11:00 AM — Adventure
Bamboo Rafting & Jungle Walk
Local operators offer bamboo rafting down the river and guided jungle walks. ~฿500–800/person.
2:00 PM
Elephant Sanctuary (Optional)
Ethical elephant sanctuaries nearby. Feed and bathe elephants — no riding. Half-day ~฿1,500–2,500/person. Book ahead.
6:00 PM
Dinner in Nimman
Return to hotel. Dinner at One Nimman food court or explore Nimman restaurants.

🎒 Packing

  • Cover shoulders & knees (temples)
  • Slip-on shoes
  • Compact umbrella
  • SPF 50+ · Insect repellent
  • Swimwear (Sticky Waterfall)
  • Light jacket (mountains are cooler)

🚗 Transport Tips

  • Private van: ฿1,500–2,500/day
  • Grab works in the city
  • Songthaew (red trucks): ฿30–60
  • Don't mix distant places in one day
  • Mountains take longer than Maps says

💡 Practical

  • Cash is king — small bills
  • Complete TDAC 72 hrs before landing
  • 7-Eleven: water, SIM cards, snacks
  • "Sawasdee" (hello) · "Khob khun" (thanks)
  • Coconut Market: Fri–Sun only
